Account & Data Deletion

How to request permanent deletion of your account and personal data.

Google Play Policy Compliance App ID: com.pdfhelper.indianformhelper Support: support@indianformhelper.app

Remember: Your documents are never on our servers

Indian Form Helper executes all document processing (photos, signatures, certificates, and PDFs) 100% on your local device. No documents or personal files are ever uploaded or stored on any server. Only optional account credentials and subscription status reside on our servers.

1. Overview & Google Play Compliance

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy on Account Deletion, if you have registered an account within the Indian Form Helper app, you have the right to request that your account and all associated data be permanently deleted.

An account in Indian Form Helper is entirely optional and exists only to sync Pro entitlements across devices. You do not need to install or keep the app installed to submit a deletion request.

2. What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Category Status Upon Deletion Details
User Account Permanently Deleted Your email address, hashed password, and user ID are purged.
Profile Information Permanently Deleted Display name and profile settings are erased immediately.
Subscription & Entitlement Permanently Deleted Pro status, expiry dates, and entitlement records are revoked.
Payment / UTR Records Permanently Deleted Manual payment verification requests and UTR references are purged.
Authentication Sessions Revoked & Destroyed All refresh tokens and active sessions are immediately invalidated.
Document Files & Photos Never Stored on Server All processing is strictly on-device. There is zero document data to delete.

Retention Policy: Once an account deletion is executed, no identifying data is retained. Deletion is irreversible.

3. How to Request Deletion

A

Method 1: In-App (Instant)

If you currently have the app installed on your phone:

  1. Open Indian Form Helper.
  2. Go to Account (or Profile → Account).
  3. Scroll to the Delete account card at the bottom.
  4. Click Delete account, type DELETE into the confirmation field.
  5. Tap Permanently delete.

Effect: Your account and server records are deleted immediately and your session is destroyed.

B

Method 2: Web / Email Request

If you uninstalled the app or cannot access your device:

Send an email request to our support team from the email address associated with your account.

Email Deletion Request

Timeline: Processed manually within 30 calendar days upon receipt.

4. On-Device Local Data & Storage Reset

Because documents are processed and saved locally on your phone, deleting your server account does not modify files saved in your phone's storage.

To erase on-device preferences and cached data:

  • In the app: Navigate to Settings → Local data & storage and select Clear History or Factory Reset.
  • In Android Settings: Go to Settings → Apps → Indian Form Helper → Storage → Clear Data & Cache.
