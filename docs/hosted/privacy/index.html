1. Introduction & The Zero-Upload Principle

Welcome to Indian Form Helper ("we", "our", or "the application"). We developed this platform specifically for candidates and applicants navigating Indian government recruitment portals (such as SSC, UPSC, State PSCs, IBPS, Railways, NTA, and defense recruitment boards) where exact file size, dimension, and privacy standards are strictly mandated.

Unlike conventional online PDF and photo compressors that upload your sensitive documents (such as Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, educational marksheets, and personal signatures) to third-party cloud servers, Indian Form Helper is engineered with a strict Zero-Knowledge, 100% Client-Side execution model.

2. How Client-Side Execution Works

When you select, drag-and-drop, or photograph a document in Indian Form Helper:

  • File Loading: The file is read directly into your device's browser/webview memory (RAM) using the standard HTML5 FileReader API.
  • Processing: Operations such as image resizing, signature background cleaning, PDF merging, password encryption, and format conversion are executed by client-side WebAssembly bytecode and HTML5 Canvas operations.
  • File Output: Processed files are generated as local binary Blobs (URL.createObjectURL) and downloaded directly to your device storage.
  • No Network Hops: At no point during file selection, processing, or downloading are the contents of your documents transmitted over the internet to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.

3. What Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect, monitor, inspect, or store any of the following:

  • Original or processed document files, photos, or images.
  • Signatures drawn on the canvas pad or extracted from photographed papers.
  • Personal details embedded within documents (e.g. candidate names, roll numbers, dates of birth, addresses, Aadhaar numbers).
  • File names, file paths, or directory structures from your device.
  • Biometric or photo metadata (EXIF data is stripped locally during processing for privacy).

4. Local Storage & Device Preferences

To provide a smooth user experience, the application stores certain non-sensitive configuration data locally on your device via Capacitor Preferences and localStorage:

  • User Profile: Your preferred display name and target government exam presets.
  • Application Settings: Your chosen theme (Light/Dark/System), default file prefix (e.g. photo_, doc_), and default output format.
  • Local History Log: If enabled, a lightweight summary (original size, output size, date, and file label) is saved locally on your device so you can track your quota and usage. The actual file bytes are not retained on our servers.
  • Custom Presets: Any custom dimension or KB requirements you save for specialized portals.

You can inspect, export, or permanently erase all local storage at any time from within the app.

5. Device Permissions

When running on mobile platforms (Android) via Capacitor, the application may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: Used exclusively if you choose to photograph a physical signature or passport photo directly within the app. Images are processed in memory and never transmitted.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to allow you to pick files for processing and save exported documents to your device downloads.

6. Third-Party Services & Monetization

The application ships with no advertising SDK of any kind. The only third party it can reach is the service that records an optional Pro subscription:

  • No advertising: There are no ads, no ad networks and no advertising identifier is read, on the free tier or any other. Nothing in the app is funded by advertising.
  • Subscriptions: If available, purchase transactions are processed securely through official Google Play billing. We do not receive or store payment card details.

7. Data Deletion & Total Reset

Because all data is stored on your device, you have complete sovereignty. You can delete all processing logs, reset presets, and wipe cache at any moment by clicking "Clear History" or "Factory Reset" in Local Data Storage, or by clearing your browser/app data.

Deleting your account

An account is optional, and only exists to carry a premium entitlement between devices. If you created one, you can delete it and everything attached to it — your email address, your entitlement and your payment history — directly from:

In-app: Navigate to Account → Delete account, type DELETE to confirm. The deletion is immediate and irrevocable.

Web / Email: You do not need the app installed to request deletion. Visit our Account Deletion Page or email support@indianformhelper.app from your registered email address. The account and all associated data will be deleted within 30 days.

Deleting an account does not touch files on your device, because nothing you process is ever uploaded to our servers.
