Terms of Use Standard software license and fair use terms

Zero Document Uploads

Indian Form Helper operates on a 100% client-side execution model. When you compress photos, edit PDFs, convert documents, or place signatures, all operations run inside your browser's local sandbox using WebAssembly and HTML5 APIs.

No Telemetry of Files

We do not log, transmit, inspect, or store your documents, photos, signatures, or metadata on any remote servers. Your data never leaves your device.

Local Preferences

Your display name, recent tools, and theme preferences are stored strictly in your device's local storage (Capacitor Preferences) and can be cleared at any time.

Understood

Use at Your Own Discretion

Indian Form Helper is provided to assist candidates in preparing photos, signatures, and documents for government exams and job portals. Always verify that your exported documents comply with specific portal guidelines before final submission.

Client-Side Reliability

All document modifications are performed locally without warranty of server uptime. Keep original backup copies of your important files before applying destructive edits.
