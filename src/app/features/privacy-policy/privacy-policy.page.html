1. Introduction & Zero-Upload Principle

Welcome to Indian Form Helper ("we", "our", or "the application"). We developed this platform specifically for candidates and applicants navigating Indian government recruitment portals (such as UPSC, SSC, State PSCs, IBPS, Railways, NTA, and defense boards) where exact file size, dimension, and privacy standards are strictly mandated.

Unlike conventional online PDF and photo compressors that upload your sensitive documents (such as Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, educational marksheets, and personal signatures) to third-party cloud servers, Indian Form Helper is engineered with a strict Zero-Knowledge, 100% Client-Side execution model.

2. How Client-Side Execution Works

When you select or drop a file into Indian Form Helper:

  • File Loading: The file is read directly into your device's browser memory (RAM) using the standard HTML5 FileReader API.
  • Processing: Operations such as image resizing, signature background cleaning, PDF merging, password encryption, and format conversion are executed by client-side WebAssembly bytecode and HTML5 Canvas operations.
  • File Output: Processed files are generated as local binary Blobs (URL.createObjectURL) and downloaded directly to your device storage.
  • No Network Hops: At no point during file selection, processing, or downloading are the contents of your documents transmitted over the internet to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.

3. What Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect, monitor, inspect, or store any of the following:

  • Original or processed document files, photos, or images.
  • Signatures drawn on the canvas pad or extracted from uploaded images.
  • Personal details embedded within documents (e.g. candidate name, roll numbers, dates of birth, addresses, Aadhaar numbers).
  • File names, file paths, or directory structures from your device.
  • Biometric or photo metadata (EXIF data is stripped locally during processing for privacy).

4. Local Storage & Device Preferences

To provide a seamless user experience, the application stores certain non-sensitive configuration data locally on your device via Capacitor Preferences and localStorage:

  • User Profile: Your preferred display name and target government exam presets.
  • Application Settings: Your chosen theme (Light/Dark/System), default file prefix (e.g. photo_, doc_), and default output format.
  • Local History Log: If enabled, a lightweight summary (original size, output size, date, and file label) is saved locally on your device so you can track your usage. The actual file bytes are not retained on our servers.
  • Custom Presets: Any custom dimension or KB requirements you save for specialized portals.

You can inspect, export, or permanently erase all local storage at any time from the Local Data Storage Manager.

5. Device Permissions

When running on mobile platforms (Android/iOS) via Capacitor, the application may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: Used exclusively if you choose to photograph a physical signature or passport photo directly within the app. Images are processed in memory and never transmitted.
  • Storage / Photo Library: Used solely to allow you to pick files for processing and save exported documents to your device.

6. Third-Party Services & Monetization

To keep our core tools freely accessible to all candidates, the application may display non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob or manage optional Pro subscriptions via RevenueCat/App Store/Play Store:

  • Ad Networks: Advertising SDKs may collect anonymous device identifiers or coarse network state in accordance with Google's privacy policies. Ad SDKs have zero access to your documents or Canvas data.
  • Pro Subscriptions: Purchase transactions are processed securely through the official Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not receive or store payment card details.

7. Data Deletion & Total Reset

Because all data is stored on your device, you have complete sovereignty. You can delete all processing logs, reset presets, and wipe cache at any moment by clicking "Clear History" or "Factory Reset" in Local Data Storage, or by clearing your browser site data.

Deleting your account

An account is optional, and only exists to carry a premium entitlement between devices. If you created one, you can delete it and everything attached to it — your email address, your entitlement and your payment history — from Account → Delete account. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

You do not need the app installed to make the request. Email support@indianformhelper.app from the address you registered with and the account will be deleted within 30 days.

Deleting an account does not touch anything on your device, because nothing you process is ever sent to us. Files you have saved stay where you saved them.

8. Google Play Data Safety Disclosure

Google Play asks every app to declare what it collects and shares. This is that declaration in full, written out so you can check it against the Data Safety label on our Play Store listing.

Your documents are never collected

Photos, signatures, PDFs, scans and every other file you open in this app are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or seen by us or by anyone else. Compression, resizing, cropping, conversion, merging, splitting, OCR, redaction and signing all run inside the app's own sandbox using WebAssembly and your device's processor. There is no server-side rendering step, no "temporary upload for processing", and no background sync of your files. Turning off the network does not stop any document tool from working — that is the simplest way to verify this for yourself.
