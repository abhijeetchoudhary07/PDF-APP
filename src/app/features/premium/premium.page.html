Payment terms

  • Billing period. Pro Monthly covers 30 days and Pro Annual 365 days from the date your payment is confirmed. Lifetime is a single payment with no expiry.
  • No automatic renewal. Nothing is charged automatically and no payment details are stored. When a plan ends, premium simply stops until you choose to pay again.
  • Activation. Premium is switched on after we confirm the transfer, usually within {{ reviewHours }} hours. You will see it unlock in the app — no code to enter.
  • Wrong amount or a payment we cannot find. We will tell you on this screen with the reason, and you can resubmit or ask us to look again. Never send a second payment before that.
  • Refunds. Contact support within 7 days of activation and we will refund to the account you paid from.
